CURRICULUM ARCHITECTURE

THE 4 CORE MODULES

Structured progression across 12 weeks. Proposed topics subject to academy confirmation

Foundations of human motion. Students learn to analyze joints as levers, calculate moment arms during lifting, and understand muscle recruitment pathways.

  • Skeletal structure & axial vs appendicular bones
  • Joint types: hinge, ball-and-socket, pivot mechanics
  • Planes of motion: Sagittal, Frontal, Transverse
  • Concentric, eccentric, and isometric contractions
  • Spine architecture & active core bracing mechanisms
  • Energy systems: Phosphagen, Glycolytic, Oxidative

Intensive gym floor lab work. Students spend extensive hours coaching each other through setup, bar paths, grip variations, and spotting protocols.

  • Squat mechanics: Back, Front, and Goblet variations
  • Hip Hinge: Conventional, Sumo, Romanian Deadlift
  • Upper Body Push: Bench press, Overhead press, Push-ups
  • Upper Body Pull: Barbell rows, Pull-ups, Lat pulldowns
  • Single Leg Drills: Split squats, Lunges, Step-ups
  • Safe spotting guidelines and gym equipment inspection

Bridging fitness with athletic rehab foundations. Learn how to screen clients for range-of-motion limitations and modify exercises to prevent discomfort.

  • Static postural evaluation & common deviations
  • Ankle dorsiflexion, hip internal/external rotation tests
  • Shoulder impingement risks & rotator cuff activation
  • Lower back fatigue management & hip mobility drills
  • Active warm-ups, dynamic stretching & foam rolling
  • Clear referral boundaries: When to direct to a physician

Transitioning from technical knowledge to working professional. Designing periodized programs and demonstrating live floor coaching competence.

  • Principles of progressive overload and volume management
  • Designing 4-week, 8-week, and 12-week client roadmaps
  • Initial client consultation & PAR-Q screening
  • Coaching verbal cues: internal vs external focus
  • Professional gym etiquette and client relationship ethics
  • Final written theory examination & live coaching evaluation
EVALUATION & CREDENTIALING

HOW YOU ARE EVALUATED

To maintain high standards, certification is earned through demonstrated competence, not merely attendance.

1. Written Examination: Covers exercise physiology, skeletal anatomy, program variables, and safety scenarios.
2. Practical Coaching Exam: Conducted on the gym floor where candidates coach a subject through randomly assigned movements and provide real-time cueing.

Notice: Successful candidates receive the Certificate in Gym Trainer Education awarded by Kynetiq Fitness + Physical Therapy Academy. We do not make unsupported claims of government affiliation or external accreditations unless verified by documentary proof.

COMMON QUERIES

FREQUENTLY ASKED QUESTIONS

Key facts about admission, eligibility, practical evaluations, and academy expectations.

What is the exact duration and batch schedule? +
The flagship Certified Gym Trainer Course runs for 3 months (12 weeks). Specific batch timings (weekday morning/evening or weekend cohorts) are finalized before each intake. Please submit an enquiry to view the calendar for the upcoming cohort.
What are the minimum eligibility criteria? +
Applicants must be at least 18 years of age, possess a foundational personal interest in fitness training, and be physically cleared to participate in gym floor coaching drills. Prior professional coaching experience is not required.
What are the tuition fees and payment options? +
Tuition details, installment schedules, and early-registration incentives are communicated directly upon enquiry. This ensures you receive the most accurate current fee structure without outdated information.
What certificate will I receive upon completion? +
Graduates receive the Certificate in Gym Trainer Education issued by Kynetiq Fitness + Physical Therapy Academy, demonstrating demonstrated competence in functional biomechanics, floor coaching, and client programming.
How much practical training is included? +
Extensive hours are dedicated directly to gym floor labs. You will practice barbell technique, spot colleagues, conduct postural screenings, and receive direct real-time feedback from our instructional staff.
